Lake Kissimmee

Big Bass Potential: ★★★★★

Big Bass Grass Shiners Structure

Starting areas:

St. Johns River

Big Bass Potential: ★★★★★

The St. Johns system contains a huge variety of bass habitat. Current, vegetation, canals, creek mouths, bends, and baitfish can create excellent ambush locations.

River Current Vegetation Structure

Starting areas:
